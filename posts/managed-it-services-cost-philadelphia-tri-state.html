By Anthony Colasante, Founder, CSuite Code

Most small businesses in the Philadelphia tri-state area pay $100 to $200 per user per month for fully managed IT services, or about $150 per hour for occasional break-fix support. A 15-person office should budget roughly $1,500 to $3,000 per month for a complete managed plan covering helpdesk, security, monitoring, and backups. Below is how pricing actually breaks down across Philadelphia, South Jersey, and Delaware - and what changes the number.

What is included in managed IT pricing?

Managed IT is a flat monthly fee that bundles your day-to-day technology operations: helpdesk support, security patching, 24/7 monitoring, backups, cybersecurity tooling, and vendor management. Instead of paying per incident, you pay per user (or per device) and everything is maintained proactively. That predictability is the entire point - one budget line instead of surprise repair bills.

Does managed IT cost more in Philadelphia than in NJ or Delaware?

Pricing across the tri-state region is broadly similar - the mid-Atlantic market runs close together - but a few local factors move the number. Philadelphia firms in regulated industries (legal, healthcare) trend toward the compliance tier. Delaware's dense finance and legal sector frequently needs SOC 2-aligned documentation, pushing toward the higher end. South Jersey pricing tracks the Philadelphia market closely given the shared metro.

What makes managed IT pricing go up?

Five factors explain almost all the variation in a quote:

  1. Compliance requirements. HIPAA, SOC 2, and similar frameworks add controls, documentation, and audit support.
  2. Security depth. EDR, SIEM, and managed detection cost more than basic antivirus - and are increasingly non-optional.
  3. On-site needs. Businesses needing regular on-site visits pay more than fully-remote-friendly ones.
  4. Number of locations. Multi-site firms (common in real estate and construction) need more networking and coordination.
  5. Legacy systems. Old servers and unsupported software cost more to keep secure and online.

Is managed IT cheaper than hiring in-house?

For most businesses under about 50 staff, yes. A single in-house IT hire in the Philadelphia area costs $70,000 to $95,000 per year plus benefits, covers only business hours, takes vacation, and is one person with one skill set. A managed plan costs a fraction of that, covers nights and weekends, and brings a whole team's range of expertise. The crossover point where in-house starts to compete is usually past 50 employees - and even then, many firms keep a managed provider for after-hours coverage and specialized security.

The bottom line on managed IT pricing

Budget $100-$200 per user per month for managed IT in the Philadelphia tri-state area, lean toward the higher end if you are in a regulated industry, and compare that against the true cost of downtime and a potential breach rather than against doing nothing. The cheapest IT plan is almost always the one that quietly prevents the incident you never had.
