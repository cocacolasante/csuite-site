Meet the Founder

Hi, I’m Anthony Colasante.

I’ve worked across multiple programming languages, modern tech stacks, AI frameworks, and cloud platforms. I started CSuite Code because too many small businesses and nonprofits get priced out of real technology advice - or worse, sold software they don’t need by a vendor whose rep gets paid on the line item.

My job is to be the technology advisor you’d hire if you could afford a full-time CTO - vendor-neutral by default, transparent about what to buy and what to skip. Where the right answer is a vendor, I’ll point you to one I’d use myself. And where you need a custom solution the market doesn’t offer, I can help build it.

No account managers, no escalation chains, no slide decks. Just an engineer who advises honestly.
