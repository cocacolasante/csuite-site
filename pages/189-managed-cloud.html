A separate division

Managed IT is structurally separate from our Tech Advisory work.

A managed services provider that also sells you the security tools, cloud platforms, and software they then implement has a conflict of interest. Keeping Managed IT separate from our Tech Advisory practice means the advice you get on what to buy stays unbiased - and the operational work stays focused.

You can engage either side independently. Most clients start with an advisory engagement, then move daily operations into our Managed IT division once the stack is right-sized.
