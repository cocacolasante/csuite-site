From Philadelphia to Pittsburgh, we serve the state’s mission-driven organizations.

Pennsylvania is home to one of the densest nonprofit ecosystems in the country. The state has thousands of 501(c)(3) organizations spanning rural healthcare networks in central PA, immigrant services in the Lehigh Valley, food banks in the steel-mill towns, education foundations in the suburbs, and arts organizations from the Allegheny to the Delaware River.

CSuite Code is based in the Greater Philadelphia region and works with nonprofits across the entire commonwealth. We work remotely with most clients, and we know PA funders and grant cycles - Pennsylvania Council on the Arts, state DCED grants, Commonwealth Charitable Trust, Heinz Endowments, Hillman Family Foundations, and the patchwork of regional community foundations.
