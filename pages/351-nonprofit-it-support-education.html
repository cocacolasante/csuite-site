Education nonprofits face a particular tech stack: student information systems (SIS), learning management systems (LMS), college and financial-aid tools, donor and grant management, family communication, and increasingly AI tutoring. On top of all that, FERPA compliance shapes how student data must be handled, stored, and shared.

CSuite Code provides nonprofit IT support sized for education organizations - from a 3-staff mentoring nonprofit to a multi-campus charter school network. We handle the day-to-day tech, the student-data privacy guardrails, and the donor/grant infrastructure that funds the mission.
