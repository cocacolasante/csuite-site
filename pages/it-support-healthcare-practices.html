What does HIPAA-compliant IT support actually require?

HIPAA-compliant IT means protected health information (ePHI) is encrypted, access is controlled and logged, backups are tested, and every vendor that touches that data signs a Business Associate Agreement (BAA). For a practice, that translates to specific technical safeguards - not a generic IT contract.

🔒

ePHI Protection & BAAs

Encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access, audit logging, and signed Business Associate Agreements - the technical safeguards HIPAA's Security Rule requires.
