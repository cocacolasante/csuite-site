Why do law and accounting firms need specialized IT support?

Professional-services firms hold highly confidential client data and bill by the hour, so downtime and a breach both cost real money. Specialized IT support means security and compliance controls - confidentiality, retention, and access - built around how the firm actually works, not a generic break-fix contract.
