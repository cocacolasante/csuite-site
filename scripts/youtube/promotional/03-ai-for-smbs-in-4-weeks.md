## Segment 2 — How we ship it in 4 weeks (0:45–1:30)

> So how do we get from zero to a working AI win in four weeks? Three steps.
>
> **Week one — workflow audit.** Thirty-minute call. I ask one question: where does your team waste time? Then I rank the answers by impact-to-effort. We pick *one*.
>
> **Weeks two and three — build.** I build the automation against your real data, your real tools. Not a demo. Not a pilot environment. The actual workflow.
>
> **Week four — ship and train.** We turn it on, your team uses it, and we watch it for a week. If it works, we plan the next one. If something needs tuning, we tune it.
