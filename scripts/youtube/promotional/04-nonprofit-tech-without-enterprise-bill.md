> I'm Anthony. I run CSuite Code. We're a fractional tech team built specifically for 501(c)(3) nonprofits — the 3-to-50 person organizations that can't justify hiring a full-time IT director and shouldn't be paying an enterprise MSP $5,000 a month.
>
> Education nonprofits, community-based social services, arts and culture, charter schools, small foundations. Especially across Pennsylvania, New Jersey, and the Philadelphia metro — though we work nationally.

**B-roll:** scrolling /nonprofits/ page → linger on the six-service grid, then on the regional service cards.

## Segment 2 — What you get (0:35–1:20)

> Here's what your nonprofit gets when you work with us.
>
> **One — discounted rates.** Verified 501(c)(3) organizations get our deepest pricing. If your operating budget is under $500K, you qualify for the deepest tier on top of that.
>
> **Two — donor-ready websites.** WordPress and custom builds with donation pages, email automation, event registration. Accessibility-compliant. Analytics wired in.
>
> **Three — IT and cloud sized for your team.** Helpdesk, security patching, backups, monitoring. Priced for a 5-person staff, not a 500-person hospital.
>
> **Four — and this is the big one — complimentary GrantMind Pro.**

**B-roll:** quick cuts of nonprofit website examples, then donor portal mockups, then the GrantMind Pro grant board.

## Segment 3 — The GrantMind hook (1:20–1:50)

> GrantMind Pro is our AI grant research and proposal-writing platform. **Normally $249 a month.** While you're an active client of CSuite Code, your team's access is included at no additional cost.
>
> It surfaces matched grants from a database of over 32,000 opportunities — refreshed daily from Grants.gov, state portals, and foundations — and AI-drafts nine standard proposal sections so your team isn't starting from a blank page.
>
> If you only got that out of the engagement — it'd already pay for itself.

**On screen:** stat overlay —
- $249 / mo included
- 32,000+ grants in database
- 9 proposal sections AI-drafted

**B-roll:** screen-record `grantmind.pro/grants/board` showing real grants with funding amounts.
